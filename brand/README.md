# Marks and wordmarks

The suite's brand files (hub D-101, ui D-18), generated by `pnpm build` from the tokens. Use these,
not copies of copies, and never edit them by hand: change `tokens/accent.json` (or
`brand/wordmarks.json`) and rebuild. Products serve the same files from `dist/brand/` (see the
package README, "Favicons").

## The rules

1. **One mark, the doll** (head and coil). Only its colour changes; never redraw, stretch, outline,
   rotate or add to it.
2. **Colour lives in the mark only.** A product's doll is its accent from the tokens, light and dark.
   One blue for the suite: `#2244FF` (dark `#6B83FF`); the older forspring.ai blue is retired.
3. **Forspring's doll is ink** (`#14151A`, paper `#EDEDEA` on dark grounds): the colourless parent;
   the products are in colour. Spring's doll is the blue (its Slack avatar); BrainSpring is part of
   Spring and uses Spring's.
4. **The wordmark is always ink** (the foreground colour), never the accent, never split by colour or
   weight: Archivo Condensed 900, written Forspring, Spring, SiteSpring, DataSpring, AppSpring,
   PulseSpring. Never lowercase, never another face.
5. **The lockup is the doll plus the wordmark**, the doll first, the name's cap height centred on it,
   12 px apart at a 56 px doll box (8 px at the bar's 28 px). In a product's
   console bar: its own doll and name, no Forspring mark or word (hub D-98). On the account: the ink
   doll, "Forspring", then the small label "konto".
6. **Clear space is a quarter of the doll's height** on every side; nothing else enters it.
   `logo.svg` and `logo-1200x300.png` carry it as their margin.
7. **The smallest doll is 16 px tall** on a screen. The 16 px favicon is the one exception and is
   cropped to the doll, so it fills the square.

## The files, in `brand/<product>/`

`<product>` is `forspring`, `spring`, `sitespring`, `dataspring`, `appspring` or `pulsespring`.

| File | What | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `mark.svg` | The doll on its own, with room around it; the dark variant inside a `prefers-color-scheme` rule | Small spaces, app icons, documents |
| `favicon.svg` | The doll cropped to itself, light and dark | The tab icon (`shell()` links it) |
| `favicon-32.png`, `favicon-16.png` | The doll, transparent | Browsers without SVG icons |
| `apple-touch-icon-180.png` | The doll on paper `#FCFCFA` | iOS home screen |
| `icon-512.png` | The doll, 512 × 512, on paper | Stripe icon, social avatars, app stores |
| `logo.svg` | Doll plus wordmark, the wordmark as outlined paths (renders without the font), light and dark, with its clear space | Headers, email, documents |
| `logo-1200x300.png` | The lockup, light, 1200 × 300, transparent, centred | Stripe logo, places that take only a PNG |

The PNGs are the light variant. On a dark ground use the SVG, which switches by itself.

`wordmarks.json` holds each name outlined from `fonts/archivo-condensed.woff2` at weight 900 with
its kerning and the bar's -0.01em tracking. A new product's name goes into `scripts/wordmarks.py`;
run it (`uv run --with fonttools --with brotli --with uharfbuzz scripts/wordmarks.py`), then
`pnpm build`.

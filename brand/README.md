# Forspring brand files

From forspring.ai (2026-10-11). Use these, not copies of copies.

| File | What | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `forspring-mark.svg` | The mark on its own | Favicons, app icons, small spaces |
| `forspring-logo.svg` | Mark plus wordmark (the wordmark is text in Archivo Condensed 800, so it needs that font to render) | Headers on the web |
| `forspring-icon-512.png` | The mark, 512 × 512, on paper `#FCFCFA` | Stripe icon, social avatars |
| `forspring-logo-1200x300.png` | Mark plus wordmark, 1200 × 300, transparent, wordmark rendered in Archivo Condensed | Stripe logo, documents, email |

The mark's blue is `#2B4BF2` as drawn on forspring.ai. The suite accent in D-90 is `#2244FF`; they are
close but not the same, and aligning them is a brand decision.
